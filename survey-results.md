# Survey results (earlier version)

An anonymous, optional survey was sent to all 84 participants of the FSE'26 study ([doi.org/10.1145/3808120](https://doi.org/10.1145/3808120)): 8 in the pilot study and 76 in the main study. 33 responded (39.3%). They rated the version of DevSelfTrace used in that study on a 5-point Likert scale. Values are percentages of the 33 responses.

Because answering was optional, the results may lean positive; because the survey was anonymous, they cannot be broken down by group. The interface has changed since (see the README), so these results describe the earlier version.
